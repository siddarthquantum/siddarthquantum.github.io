श्री हनुमान चालीसा

Shri Hanuman Chalisa • Devotional & Artistic Experience

Doha 1
श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज निज मनु मुकुर सुधारि।
बरनउँ रघुबर बिमलु जोसु दायकु फल चारि॥
Cleansing the mirror of my mind with the dust of my Guru's lotus feet, I invoke the pure fame of Lord Rama, which bestows the fourfold fruits of life.
01
जय हनुमान ज्ञान गुन सागर।
जय कपीस तिहुँ लोक उजागर॥
Victory to Lord Hanuman, ocean of wisdom and virtue! Victory to the lord of vanaras, who illuminates the three worlds.
