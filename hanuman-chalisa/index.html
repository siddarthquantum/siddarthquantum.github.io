श्री हनुमान चालीसा

Shri Hanuman Chalisa • Devotional & Artistic Experience

दोहाDoha

Doha 1
श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज, निज मनु मुकुरु सुधारि।
बरनउँ रघुबर बिमल जसु, जो दायकु फल चारि॥
Cleansing the mirror of my mind with the dust of my Guru's lotus feet, I sing the pure glory of Shri Rama, which bestows the four fruits of life.
Doha 2
बुद्धिहीन तनु जानिके, सुमिरौं पवन-कुमार।
बल बुधि बिद्या देहु मोहिं, हरहु कलेस बिकार॥
Knowing myself to be without wisdom, I remember you, son of the Wind. Grant me strength, wisdom and knowledge, and remove my afflictions and impurities.

चौपाईChaupai

01
जय हनुमान ज्ञान गुन सागर।
जय कपीस तिहुँ लोक उजागर॥
Victory to Hanuman, ocean of wisdom and virtue! Victory to the lord of the vanaras, who illumines the three worlds.
02
राम दूत अतुलित बल धामा।
अंजनि-पुत्र पवनसुत नामा॥
You are Rama's messenger, abode of matchless strength, known as Anjani's son and son of the Wind.
03
महाबीर बिक्रम बजरंगी।
कुमति निवार सुमति के संगी॥
Great hero, mighty and valiant, strong as a thunderbolt: you drive away evil thoughts and are the companion of good sense.
04
कंचन बरन बिराज सुबेसा।
कानन कुंडल कुंचित केसा॥
Your complexion is golden and you are beautifully dressed, with earrings in your ears and curly hair.
05
हाथ बज्र औ ध्वजा बिराजै।
काँधे मूँज जनेऊ साजै॥
In your hands shine the thunderbolt and the banner; the sacred thread of munja grass adorns your shoulder.
06
संकर सुवन केसरीनंदन।
तेज प्रताप महा जग बंदन॥
Embodiment of Shiva and joy of Kesari, your radiance and glory are revered by the whole world.
07
बिद्यावान गुनी अति चातुर।
राम काज करिबे को आतुर॥
Learned, virtuous and supremely wise, you are always eager to do Rama's work.
08
प्रभु चरित्र सुनिबे को रसिया।
राम लखन सीता मन बसिया॥
You delight in hearing the Lord's story; Rama, Lakshmana and Sita dwell in your heart.
09
सूक्ष्म रूप धरि सियहिं दिखावा।
बिकट रूप धरि लंक जरावा॥
Taking a tiny form you appeared before Sita; taking a fearsome form you set Lanka ablaze.
10
भीम रूप धरि असुर सँहारे।
रामचंद्र के काज सँवारे॥
Taking a mighty form you destroyed the demons and fulfilled Ramachandra's tasks.
11
लाय सजीवन लखन जियाये।
श्रीरघुबीर हरषि उर लाये॥
You brought the Sanjivani herb and revived Lakshmana; Shri Raghubir embraced you with joy.
12
रघुपति कीन्ही बहुत बड़ाई।
तुम मम प्रिय भरतहि सम भाई॥
Raghupati praised you greatly: “You are as dear to me as my brother Bharata.”
13
सहस बदन तुम्हरो जस गावैं।
अस कहि श्रीपति कंठ लगावैं॥
“May a thousand mouths sing your glory” — saying this, the Lord held you close.
14
सनकादिक ब्रह्मादि मुनीसा।
नारद सारद सहित अहीसा॥
Sanaka and the sages, Brahma and the great munis, Narada, Saraswati and Shesha, king of serpents…
15
जम कुबेर दिगपाल जहाँ ते।
कबि कोबिद कहि सके कहाँ ते॥
…Yama, Kubera and the guardians of the directions cannot fully describe your glory; how then could poets and scholars?
16
तुम उपकार सुग्रीवहिं कीन्हा।
राम मिलाय राज पद दीन्हा॥
You did Sugriva a great service: you brought him to Rama and restored his kingdom.
17
तुम्हरो मंत्र बिभीषन माना।
लंकेस्वर भए सब जग जाना॥
Vibhishana followed your counsel and became the king of Lanka, as the whole world knows.
18
जुग सहस्र जोजन पर भानू।
लील्यो ताहि मधुर फल जानू॥
The sun, thousands of yojanas away, you swallowed, taking it for a sweet fruit.
19
प्रभु मुद्रिका मेलि मुख माहीं।
जलधि लाँघि गये अचरज नाहीं॥
Holding the Lord's ring in your mouth you leapt across the ocean — no wonder in that.
20
दुर्गम काज जगत के जेते।
सुगम अनुग्रह तुम्हरे तेते॥
Every difficult task in the world becomes easy by your grace.
21
राम दुआरे तुम रखवारे।
होत न आज्ञा बिनु पैसारे॥
You are the guardian of Rama's door; no one enters without your permission.
22
सब सुख लहै तुम्हारी सरना।
तुम रच्छक काहू को डर ना॥
Those who take refuge in you find every happiness; with you as protector there is nothing to fear.
23
आपन तेज सम्हारो आपै।
तीनों लोक हाँक तें काँपै॥
You alone can contain your own power; the three worlds tremble at your roar.
24
भूत पिसाच निकट नहिं आवै।
महाबीर जब नाम सुनावै॥
Ghosts and evil spirits do not come near when the name Mahavir is spoken.
25
नासै रोग हरै सब पीरा।
जपत निरंतर हनुमत बीरा॥
Disease is destroyed and all pain removed by constantly chanting the name of brave Hanuman.
26
संकट तें हनुमान छुड़ावै।
मन क्रम बचन ध्यान जो लावै॥
Hanuman frees from every trouble those who remember him in thought, deed and word.
27
सब पर राम तपस्वी राजा।
तिन के काज सकल तुम साजा॥
Rama, the ascetic king, reigns over all, and you accomplished all his work.
28
और मनोरथ जो कोई लावै।
सोइ अमित जीवन फल पावै॥
Whoever comes to you with any wish receives the boundless fruit of life.
29
चारों जुग परताप तुम्हारा।
है परसिद्ध जगत उजियारा॥
Your glory spans all four ages; your light is famed throughout the world.
30
साधु संत के तुम रखवारे।
असुर निकंदन राम दुलारे॥
You are the protector of saints and sages, destroyer of demons, beloved of Rama.
31
अष्ट सिद्धि नौ निधि के दाता।
अस बर दीन जानकी माता॥
You can bestow the eight siddhis and nine treasures — this boon was granted by Mother Janaki.
32
राम रसायन तुम्हरे पासा।
सदा रहो रघुपति के दासा॥
You hold the elixir of devotion to Rama; may you ever remain the servant of Raghupati.
33
तुम्हरे भजन राम को पावै।
जनम जनम के दुख बिसरावै॥
Through devotion to you one reaches Rama and forgets the sorrows of many lifetimes.
34
अंत काल रघुबर पुर जाई।
जहाँ जन्म हरि-भक्त कहाई॥
At life's end one goes to Raghubar's abode, and wherever born again is known as a devotee of Hari.
35
और देवता चित्त न धरई।
हनुमत सेइ सर्ब सुख करई॥
One need hold no other deity in mind; serving Hanuman brings every happiness.
36
संकट कटै मिटै सब पीरा।
जो सुमिरै हनुमत बलबीरा॥
Troubles end and all pain vanishes for those who remember the mighty, brave Hanuman.
37
जै जै जै हनुमान गोसाईं।
कृपा करहु गुरुदेव की नाईं॥
Victory, victory, victory to Lord Hanuman! Bless me as my Guru would.
38
जो सत बार पाठ कर कोई।
छूटहि बंदि महा सुख होई॥
Whoever recites this a hundred times is freed from bondage and attains great bliss.
39
जो यह पढ़ै हनुमान चालीसा।
होय सिद्धि साखी गौरीसा॥
Whoever reads this Hanuman Chalisa attains fulfilment; Lord Shiva himself is witness.
40
तुलसीदास सदा हरि चेरा।
कीजै नाथ हृदय महँ डेरा॥
Tulsidas, forever the servant of Hari, prays: O Lord, make your dwelling in my heart.

दोहाDoha

Doha
पवनतनय संकट हरन, मंगल मूरति रूप।
राम लखन सीता सहित, हृदय बसहु सुर भूप॥
O son of the Wind, remover of troubles, embodiment of all that is auspicious: with Rama, Lakshmana and Sita, dwell in my heart, O king of the gods.
